# Feature handoff

- Full authorized objective and canonical specification; product gates stay open.
- Exact checkpoint branch/SHA supplied in successor prompt, or hashed snapshot.
- Accepted feature behavior, relevant changed files and actual validation.
- Confirmed defects, unavailable capabilities, untested work and remaining gates.
- One next concrete feature: owned paths, completion criteria and first actions.
- Exact owned checkout path supplied privately; do not use a busy default checkout.
- Required skills, durable constraints, active processes and their owners.
- Workers: integrated work and separately preserved unaccepted changes/branches.
- Private companion and unique acceptance receipt paths supplied privately.
- Successor confirms its own actual full ACTIVE goal and owned branch/HEAD/remote
  before edits; new `gpt-6.1-sol` / `high` chat, then continues.
- One small receipt: accepted, threadId, branch, commit, nextFeature, handoffsRead,
  goalStatus, goalScope, ownershipAccepted. It transfers exclusive ownership.
- Link historical evidence in place. No recursive ancestor audit, global ref-count
  equality, startupack/closure sequence or unrelated-checkout gate for transport.
- Archive only predecessor after matching receipt. Carry the full goal forward.
